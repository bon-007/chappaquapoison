The case file that arrived in her chambers contained the accumulated weight of years — the supervised visits, the bruises, the ambush, the poisoning evidence, the defaults, the recusals, the memoranda, the restraining orders from California, the laboratory reports, the custody orders built on a foundation that kept shifting.

Schauer did two things.

She vacated the Horowitz default. The procedural ruling that had been entered when Steve arrived at a closed door was undone. The default that had shaped years of custody proceedings — the ruling that existed because a remote appearance request was denied at five o'clock the evening before — was removed from the record.

The default was removed from the record.


The second thing Schauer did was not grant Steve's restraining order.

The California DVRO existed. The two defaults by Tara existed. The poisoning evidence existed. The TOP had been granted at the first hearing. The evidentiary record supported a permanent order of protection.

Instead Schauer issued a mutual temporary restraining order.

A mutual order creates symmetrical restrictions — each side restrained equally.


The court had corrected the error.


The case had been reset. The procedural error was undone. But the reset did not return the visits that had been lost. It did not address the poisoning. It did not examine the supervision manipulation or the ambush or the bruises or the disappeared reports. It reset the procedure and left everything else exactly where it was.

The case had been reset. The clock returned to zero. Evie was older now. Every procedural reset consumed months. Every month was time that could not be recovered.

Kelly had watched all of it. She had watched Gordon-Oliver recuse. She had watched Horowitz enter a default on a manufactured absence. She had watched Humphrey recuse after Tara's two no-shows produced no consequence. Now she watched Schauer vacate the default and issue a mutual order — treating the two sides as equivalent.

Four judges. Not the same rulings, but the same outcome: nothing changed for Evie. The file got thicker. The child got older. And each new judge started from zero.

Kelly had been writing. The visit write-ups. The letters to the review board. The documentation she kept building because someone had to keep building it.

The file was reassigned. The mutual order was issued. The case continued.
