REFERENCE: Spawn (1997) — The Clown/Violator Character

John Leguizamo played the Clown/Violator in the 1997 Spawn movie. The character 
is a grotesque, overweight demon disguised as a short, fat clown with wild curly 
hair, bad skin, and a mocking personality. Eight hours of prosthetic makeup.

CONNECTION TO JESSE OZERI:
Tara placed a Spawn Clown/Violator action figure in the refrigerator, knowing 
Jesse — who had a significant weight problem and very curly hair — would go 
looking for snacks at night. When he found the figure that cruelly resembled him, 
he was very upset. 

This incident demonstrates:
1. Tara's pattern of deploying knowledge of a target's specific vulnerability
2. Physical cruelty disguised as humor (same mechanism as the poisoning)
3. The trap structure: weapon placed where only the target will find it
4. Cruelty directed at the person who loves her most

Source: Steve Russell (author interview, Session 18 reading)
