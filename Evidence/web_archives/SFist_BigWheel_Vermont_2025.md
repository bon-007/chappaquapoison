## Key Facts

Vermont Street in Potrero Hill is San Francisco's true crookedest street — sinuosity 1.56 vs Lombard's 1.2, as measured by Travel Channel's "Fact or Fiction." Originally a straight unpaved road, switchbacks were added circa 1928 to make the steep incline navigable.

The Bring Your Own Big Wheel race began in 2000 when John Brumit rode a children's big wheel down Lombard Street. The event moved to Vermont Street in 2009. By 2025, it was in its 23rd year, held on Easter Sunday.
