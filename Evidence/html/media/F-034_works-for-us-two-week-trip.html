Overview: A Carefully Arranged Disappearance

This post documents what would become one of the most critical events in the ChappaquaPoison case: a two-week "vacation" to New York that was arranged ostensibly as therapeutic intervention but ultimately served as the mechanism for a coordinated kidnapping of Evie from California. The title "Works for Us" captures the seemingly reasonable agreement made by Stephen Russell to facilitate the trip—an agreement that would be catastrophically violated.

The Arrangement

After Tara's second attempt to flee the state with Evie, a concerned Stephen Russell entered into discussions with her family and doctors. They jointly agreed that a two-to-three week vacation to New York would be therapeutically beneficial for Tara, provided she:

  • Was supervised by her parents
  • Was monitored by her doctors during the trip
  • Returned in time for the upcoming court hearing

Russell's Good Faith Participation

Stephen Russell:

  • Arranged for the nanny to travel with Tara and Evie
  • Reached out directly to Steve Walsh Sr. to coordinate trip details
  • Confirmed the arrangement and its conditions
  • Sent confirmation messages documenting the agreement
"Dad arranges for the nanny to travel with Mom and reaches out to the Steve Walsh to update him on the details of the trip. 'Works for us,' he replies."

Steve Walsh Sr.'s simple response—"Works for us"—appeared to confirm his understanding and agreement to the conditions. This response would later be pivotal evidence of the family's coordinated deception.

Tara's Confirmations

Even more damning, Tara herself sent multiple confirmations of her understanding:

  • First confirmation: Mom confirms the agreement on her flight to New York with Evie and her nanny
  • Second confirmation: Mom confirms her understanding of the agreement again on her flight to New York

These multiple confirmations from Tara herself would later demonstrate premeditation—she had multiple opportunities to clarify the arrangement and instead repeatedly confirmed her understanding.

The Betrayal: The Kidnapping

What Stephen Russell agreed to as a temporary, supervised two-to-three week therapeutic vacation became a permanent removal of the child from California. The Walsh family:

  • Never returned Tara and Evie to California
  • Removed the child from the jurisdiction in violation of custody orders
  • Created a situation where Russell could not see his daughter for extended periods
  • Demonstrated the family's willingness to blatantly violate agreements and court orders

Evidence of Premeditation

The coordinated nature of this kidnapping is evidenced by:

  • The false agreement to return by a specific date
  • Multiple confirmations by Tara of the limited-duration arrangement
  • The presence of the nanny who would later testify to witnessing drugging
  • The careful coordination with Steve Walsh Sr. to discuss "final vacation details"
  • The execution with apparent agreement and without suspicion from Russell

Pattern of Escalation

This kidnapping represents an escalation in the family's conduct:

  • Previous: Covert drugging of Russell
  • Previous: Attempts to have Russell committed to psychiatric care
  • Now: Coordinated kidnapping of the child from the jurisdiction
  • Later: Efforts to keep Evie from Russell through custody manipulation

Legal Significance

This event became central to litigation because:

  • It constituted kidnapping/parental abduction
  • It violated California custody orders
  • It demonstrated the family's willingness to engage in serious crimes
  • It created the circumstances for the subsequent New York litigation
  • The documented nature of the false agreement proved premeditation

Title Significance: "Works for Us"

The title captures the grim irony of the situation. Stephen Russell thought it "worked" for him to allow Tara and Evie to go to New York for therapeutic purposes. The Walsh family's response "Works for us" carried a different, sinister meaning—it worked for them because it allowed them to kidnap Evie and remove her from Russell's custody entirely.
