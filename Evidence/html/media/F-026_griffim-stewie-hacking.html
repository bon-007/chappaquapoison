Overview

This evidence documents the pattern of computer hacking, impersonation, and betrayal by Brendan Walsh, Tara's younger brother, as reported by his sister Brienne Walsh (Aunt Brie) in her contemporaneous blog from May 2010. The post provides documentation of behavior patterns that would later be noted as relevant to family dysfunction and the overall pattern of deception within the Walsh household.

Documented Aliases and Characterizations

According to Aunt Brie's account, Brendan Walsh was known by multiple names within the family dynamic:

  • "Stewie" - A reference to Stewie Griffin from the cartoon Family Guy, noting his perceived genius-level intelligence and troublemaking nature
  • "Stuprendan" - A family nickname combining "stupid" with his name
  • "Dexter" - A reference to his fascination with blood, weapons, and his ambulance-chasing behavior

Specific Incidents of Computer Abuse and Impersonation

"Stuprendan is what some may call a child genius. Not infrequently, people have referred to him as Stewie Griffin from Family Guy. Recently it was unveiled that Stewprendan has been betraying me on a number of fronts. He hacked into my email account."

Email Account Hacking

Brendan gained unauthorized access to his sister's email account, demonstrating technical sophistication and a willingness to violate her privacy. This was not an isolated incident but part of a pattern Aunt Brie discovered.

Photo Theft and Public Humiliation

Brendan obtained private photographs from the family home and showed them to his mother, despite the fact that their mother "doesn't know how to use the Internet." The photographs had been posted on Aunt Brie's private blog. This action served to:

  • Violate his sister's privacy
  • Embarrass her in front of their mother
  • Demonstrate collusion with family authority figures to undermine her autonomy

Impersonation of Family Members

"He frequently IMs me and pretends to be my grandmother."

Brendan engaged in online impersonation of his grandmother, creating false communications to deceive his sister. This represents a pattern of deliberate deception and psychological manipulation.

Deliberate Communication Sabotage

"Last week, he 'forgot' to tell my parents that I was coming up from the city, and then screened my desperate phone calls from the train station."

In a particularly troubling incident, Brendan deliberately sabotaged his sister's attempts to communicate with her family. He:

  • Failed to inform parents of her arrival (claiming to have "forgotten")
  • Answered her incoming phone calls but screened them, blocking her communication
  • Left her stranded at a train station unable to reach her family

Aunt Brie's Response and Interpretation

"Stewprendan, you are my little brother. I love you. Why must you betray me?"

Aunt Brie's conclusion, while expressing familial affection, explicitly characterizes Brendan's actions as a pattern of betrayal. She recognizes these behaviors as deliberately harmful despite their family relationship.

Broader Significance to the Walsh Family Pattern

This 2010 documentation is significant because it demonstrates:

  • Early manifestation of dysfunctional behavior: These incidents occurred in 2010, years before the events that led to the ChappaquaPoison litigation
  • Technological sophistication and criminal potential: Hacking skills used for family manipulation
  • Family collusion: Parents' apparent acceptance or tolerance of Brendan's actions
  • Pattern of deception: Multiple forms of lying and impersonation used against family members
  • Isolation and control tactics: Deliberate sabotage of communication and relationships

Connection to Later Events

In later events described in the ChappaquaPoison narratives, Brendan Walsh would be involved in the September 2019 incident at the Walsh compound, where he and cousin Brian Meenan waited outside visitation pickup locations. The pattern of behavior documented in this 2010 post—deception, manipulation, and willingness to act against family members—provides context for understanding his later actions in obstruction of the supervised visitation process.
