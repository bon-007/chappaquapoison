- Spelling not confirmed or corrected — flag remains for verification. May appear in iMessage archive or flight records.

- The text to Saoira is significant: "This is what I came home to [picture of me with Evie] It's better here." Tara photographed Steve with their daughter and sent it to a friend as proof of her domestic life. This is performance — curating the image of a family for an audience. The picture is real (Steve with Evie). The framing is Tara's narrative: "It's better here" implies she had a choice, that she came home to something good.
- This text may exist in the iMessage archives — search for Saoira or the phrase "came home to" or "better here."
- For the prose: Saoira doesn't need to be explained. She's a walk-on. But the text to her is a window into Tara's relationship to her own life — she documented it for others in real time. The archive instinct wasn't only Steve's.
