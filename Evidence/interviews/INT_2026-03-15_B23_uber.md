## Transcript Summary

### On Bryan Crutcher's State During the Uber
**Q:** [Flagged two interpretive lines in B23 rewrite — "He was not panicking. He was managing" about Walsh Sr.]
**Steve:** "If this was about that moment, it was surreal. Bryan was panicking. The stakes were clearly high so I think I went to a place of calm competence. I didn't let myself feel the moment. Instead I contacted people that might know what to do and centered myself for what was next."

**Classification:** Uncorroborated but specific. Author's direct testimony about his emotional state and Bryan's. Consistent with the operational response pattern (calling Snyder and Poole immediately).

### On the Decision: Arrest vs. Family Court
**Q:** [Follow-up on Steve's response to Bryan's text]
**Steve:** "I called Sean Snyder and Stacey Poole. The answer was found quickly. You can't kidnap a kid. The question was really for Tara, did I have her arrested or did we do a family court action and that's what we chose to do. We anchored and deescalated."

**Classification:** Uncorroborated but specific. Author testimony about deliberate choice. The filing record confirms family court action (DVRO filed July 9, no arrest record).

### On the Timing of the Court Order
**Q:** [B23 rewrite assumed ATROs preceded the kidnapping attempt]
**Steve:** "When she first kidnaps Evie, I don't think the full court order exists yet. I had been slow in prosecuting the criminal and custodial claims. It's only when Tara is actively kidnapping Evie that I assert myself. The Order that stops her is a reaction to her kidnapping attempt it didn't exist before."

**Classification:** Partially corroborated. Archive shows Parentage Petition filed June 4 (one day before Uber), Tara served June 5 morning, Uber departure June 5 afternoon. Steve's memory that the order was reactive is confirmed by the sequence — the petition triggered ATROs, and Tara was served the same day she tried to leave. Emergency custody order came July 9-10, over a month later.

### On Evie Falling Off the Bed
**Q:** [Steve raised this unprompted in context of the service timeline]
**Steve:** "I remember it being after she was served she ran into our bedroom not wanting to go to other apt. She left Evie on bed and came back to hold door closed and Evie rolled off bed onto floor and had to go to emergency room."

**Classification:** Corroborated with date correction. Archive (Tedla Declaration, trial testimony ExH_03) places this incident on May 15, 2018 — three weeks before the Uber, not the day of service. Steve's memory of the event details matches the archive precisely (Tara pushes Steve out of bedroom, door closes, Evie falls, ER visit). His memory fused the service date with the bedroom incident — both involve Tara reacting violently to perceived loss of control.

### On Tara's Gun Hallucination and Bathroom Phone Call
**Q:** "Do you know the date that Tara called me into bathroom to talk to her Mom bc Tara realized she had been hallucinating that I was chasing her with a gun?"
**Steve:** [Question was Steve's own — he asked for the date]

**Classification:** Corroborated. Archive places this in mid-May 2018. Trial testimony (ExH_03): Steve testified Tara "called me into the bathroom and handed me the phone, and it was her mother. Tara had told her mother that she had a delusional episode." Tara's own texts (WALSH_005693, May 17): "I seriously don't think Steve ever had a gun it was all on my head I made up the whole thing even the locks — no of it is real... I need to go see my doctor ASAP."

### On the Vacation Decision — The Asymmetric Orientation
**Q:** [Steve raised this unprompted after reviewing the May-June timeline]
**Steve:** "I think something that might be missing but evident from the Evidence, is that I made a mistake letting Tara go home for vacation and to see her doctor. I was exhausted and scared and weak and wanted relief from this moment, but bc Maura had reached out to me to get her daughter a higher standard of care after the gun delusion, I thought we might be a kind of family all on the same team working through something. I couldn't square a family that had entrusted me with their daughter's care vs someone that was going to lie to betray my trust and kidnap my daughter."

**Classification:** Corroborated. Archive confirms: (1) June 8 email exchange — Steve agreed to 2-3 week vacation conditional on Tara seeing NY therapist about BPD, provided round-trip tickets; (2) Walsh Sr. replied "Works for us — I appreciate your flexibility Steve"; (3) June 9 — Tara departed with Evie; (4) She never returned. The vacation ruse is documented in the Master Timeline (Entries 46-47) and the 165EvidencePoints document calls it "The 'Works for Us' Trap."

### On the Asymmetric Orientation Pattern
**Steve:** "It's also worth capturing how the Walshes wish everyone to be sick and I keep holding out for the fact that they might be well."

**Classification:** Author directive. Captured as new thematic lens (L-023) in V3_THEMATIC_MEMORY.md and as structural motif in NARRATIVES_AND_THEMES.md.

### Key Correction
The B23 rewrite originally compressed the emergency custody order into the evening of June 5. The archive shows the DVRO petition and emergency custody orders were filed July 9-10, 2018 — over a month later. The Parentage Petition (June 4) with its ATROs was the legal mechanism in place on June 5, not a separate emergency order.
