### Transcript Summary

**Q: Walk me through what happened when Evie arrived for the visit.**

Steve: Evie arrived that day and we were already on guard. Everything had been a deliberate challenge. Up to now Evie had always arrived unfed or without a nap or after a dramatic handoff that saw her amped up mirroring Maura's energy and upset. On this day, we were prepared. She arrived upset. We had a nanny, a supervisor, myself present and Kelly in next room. We immediately did the normal triage. We changed her and gave her something to eat. She immediately was better after a little fruit and sugar babyfood packet. But upon changing her we noticed a lot of bruising and it looked weird.

**Q: Describe the bruises specifically.**

Steve: Just where her diaper line met her leg were two deep bruises that looked like a pinch mark. The bruises were finger width apart and the bruise where the thumb would be was bigger than the other. On her shin were 4 symmetrical bruises. Very symmetrical like she had been hit with the same object 4 times, like a spoon or a rod.

**Q: Who noticed the bruises?**

Steve: I noticed it, the nanny noticed it, the supervisor noticed it. [Three adults independently observed the injuries.]

**Q: Who did you contact first?**

Steve: We contacted my lawyer and the AFC Jennifer Jackman right away. Prob my attorney first.

**Q: What happened with the visit after the discovery?**

Steve: We focused on the visit and Evie bounced back after a nap and some food. She was exhausted and starving and returned to her happy self after some food and rest.

**Q: What was Jennifer Jackman's response?**

Steve: I'd have to look at the emails, but I think Jennifer Jackman responded that Tara had reported bruises that were concerning after the visit and it was almost as if I was being cautioned to let it go. But armed with the fact that both Tara and I found the bruises alarming the obvious culprit was the Walshes. They had a history of abusing and drugging children, so I called the police.

**Q: What happened with the police?**

Steve: The police were great and responded but told me that the Walsh grandparents had said the bruises were normal even though both Tara and I reported this as obviously abnormal.

**Q: What happened with Tara's attorney?**

Steve: Tara's attorney then wrote a letter saying something very different than what Jackman said Tara told her. He said there were no bruises and that I was a liar and made the whole thing up. The court went with that version, though no evidence was offered and it contradicted all evidence and testimony.

**Q: Do you remember which nanny or supervisor was present?**

Steve: I don't remember which nanny or supervisor it was. The answers would be in the Evie Books or supervisor reports or Nanny Talia's affidavits.

**Q: Do you have photographs of the actual bruises?**

Steve: I don't know if we have or can use an actual photo of the abuse, but we did do a contemporaneous image marking the bruise locations and that image is in one of the Evie Story volumes. [Identified as the body diagram in SLE-051.]

**Q: Where is the Jackman email?**

Steve: We have my email to Jackman and her response in the Mail directory in the Work folder.

### Supplemental Interview — Talia Kleiman Naming & Walsh Driveway Confrontation

**Q: Is Talia someone who could be named publicly in the blog, or are there reasons to keep her anonymous?**

Steve: Talia was in the car and non-visible at the drop-off of Evie. Steve pulled in behind us. Blocking us in. He came at me red-faced, exaggerated, as if to create conflict. It felt staged. When he saw tiny Talia sitting low in the passenger seat he froze and retreated to his car. It made it seem to me obvious as a kind of set-up. We published a post that called him "Red faced and angry." Tara had some quote that was something like "dad is really pissed, he's going after Steve now." I started calling Steve Walsh "Steve Walsh the coward" after that because I got a chance to look into his soul and see the real person that he was.

**Interview Note:** Steve did not directly answer the naming question but freely used Talia's full name and described her as a witness at the scene. The SLE blog (SLE-053) already published the scene with the nanny visible in the car. The Master Evidence Archive names Talia Kleiman and includes her written statement. Her name appears in the existing B27 post. **Assessment: Talia Kleiman is a named, documented witness in the public record and can be named in the blog.**

**Post-Interview Research:** The driveway confrontation Steve describes matches SLE-053 (Visit 15, Part 3, published April 1, 2019) exactly. Key corroborating details:

- SLE-053: "Steve Walsh jumped out of his car, again shaking. His face red with rage, he strode up toward Dad's parked car. A few steps from the window, he stopped noticing for the first-time the petite nanny in the passenger seat."
- SLE-053: "'You...uh...you shouldn't be blocking my driveway,' he stammered back-stepping toward the car."
- SLE-053: "It was Sunday." — contradicting Walsh's claim he was "taking the girls home from school."
- SLE-053: "The Nanny would write a letter to the court about the incident."
- SLE-059 (March 23, earlier confrontation): "Mom's sister's text messages would show Steve Walsh became furious after talking to the police." — this may be the source of the "dad is really pissed" quote Steve recalls.
- Steve Walsh had been warned by police twice not to be present at Evie's pickups and drop-offs.
- This confrontation happened THE SAME DAY as the bruise discovery (March 31, 2019). The sequence: Evie arrives with bruises → visit → Steve returns Evie to Walsh compound → Steve Walsh Sr. confronts Steve at the driveway.
- Steve's same-day email (3:28 PM) also documents "Steve Walsh Sr. following them after the visit despite police warnings."

**Relevance to B27:** The driveway confrontation is part of the same day as the bruises. It demonstrates the Walsh family's escalating intimidation pattern on the very day bruises were discovered on Evie. Potential inclusion as a scene in B27 or as a bridge to the next post.

### Supplemental Interview — Walsh Family Background: AA, Anger Management, Michael

**Q (implied): What did you know about the Walsh family's history before the bruises?**

Steve: When I met Steve Walsh, Tara told me that he and Maura were in AA and that Steve had been in anger management. The actual reason he had to go to anger management was a mystery, but meeting Michael I remember wondering if he had been abused. He was a tall and beautiful young man and the retardation seemed to me as a non-expert as something resulting from trauma and not genetics.

**Post-Interview Research — Corroboration:**

1. **AA disclosure confirmed** — B04 (Tara Knoll, Easter dinner scene): "Her parents were in AA. Both of them. They had done the twelve steps — the moral inventory, the amends, the structured atonement that the program requires. They had been in anger management." This matches Steve's testimony exactly. Tara herself disclosed this to Steve.

2. **Anger management confirmed** — SLE-047 ("Grumpa Struggles with Uncontrollable Rage & Anger Management") documents Walsh Sr.'s rage as a pattern, not a single incident. SLE-059 confirms: "He had entered AA and anger management after Mom sued for emancipation and her attorney called Child Protective Services on him more than 30 times."

3. **Reason for anger management** — The SLE blog connects it directly to Tara's emancipation suit and CPS calls. The "mystery" Steve describes is the family's version — they disclosed AA and anger management but not the trigger (their daughter suing to escape them, their daughter's attorney calling CPS 35+ times). SLE-072 title: "CPS Called on Grimma and Grumpa Over 35 Times."

4. **Michael Walsh** — B04 documents Michael at the Easter dinner: described as the family's "first son, their first attempt," with a developmental disability the family "managed but did not explain." Steve's observation that the disability appeared trauma-related rather than genetic is not corroborated or contradicted by any medical evidence in the archive. However, the family's documented pattern of physical abuse (Brienne's sworn deposition: "We were hit"; Brienne's blog: Maura digging nails into forearms; Kiara's 2017 claim of being drugged with Lithium and Seroquel; CPS called 35+ times) establishes that violence and drugging were present across generations of Walsh children. Steve's instinct about Michael — that what he was seeing was the result of something done, not something inherited — aligns with the documented pattern but cannot be confirmed from available evidence.

5. **The matching outfits** — The two adopted Korean sisters (Kiara and Mariah) in matching outfits appear in SLE-053 during the driveway confrontation: Walsh Sr. "retreated toward his car where his two youngest daughters sat, dressed per usual in matching outfits." SLE-111 documents this as a community concern.

**Significance for B27:** Steve's testimony establishes that he entered the Walsh family with Tara's own disclosures about her parents' alcoholism and anger issues — and with his own first-person observation of Michael's condition. When he discovered bruises on Evie years later, he was not inventing a narrative about family violence. The family had told him about their history. He had seen its effects on Michael. The bruises confirmed what the family itself had disclosed and what multiple institutions had documented across decades.

### Supplemental Interview — Griffin Credential Fraud & Ackerman FBI Background

**On Griffin's false institutional affiliation:**

Steve: He says he is from the Greenwich Center for Addiction and Recovery or Recovery and Addiction. Whatever he says, the opposite actually exists in Greenwich, but when I call them they have no record of Griffin. Which is odd because Griffin says he's the CEO of the Greenwich Center for..., but what is doubly odd is that they like the court confronted with his fraud have just never thought about it or heard about it. It seems impossible that I am the first person in 30 years to do such a cursory investigation.

**Interview Note:** This is unindexed testimony. The canonical index documents Griffin's CASAC credential (#1636), its expiration (Feb 2018), and its revocation (Aug 2019). But the specific claim about the Greenwich center name discrepancy — that the center exists under a slightly different name and has no record of Griffin — is not captured in any exhibit. Steve's phone call is a primary-source investigation that should be documented.

**On Ackerman's FBI background and attorney:**

Steve: When Don Ackerman was caught I looked him up and he was kind of an important guy in FBI history, someone who found the Unabomber or something. And his attorney that called me was like a super NY big wig with connections to the Gov or someone. It really made me feel like there was conspiracy... why were so many powerful and connected people doing exactly the wrong thing?

**Post-Interview Research:** Confirmed. Donald J. Ackerman: 32-year FBI career, supervised the Unabomber Task Force as Bay Area divisional head. Now President/CEO of Ackerman & Associates, SVP at Risk Control Strategies, New York. Voicemail from his attorney in archive (ExMM_VM_Don_Ackerman_Attorney.wav). Attorney not yet identified by name.
