# INT-003-001 — Tara Before the Poisoning

**Date:** March 15, 2026
**Subject:** Stephen Russell
**Interviewer:** Claude (ChappaquaPoison v3 editorial)
**Question:** What was Tara like when you met her? What made you stay? The narrative has moral complexity as a principle, but right now Tara reads as an antagonist from page one. I need the other side of her to write her honestly.

---

**Answer (verbatim):**

Tara was to me a bit of a puzzle. I saw her as a "best and brightest" in disguise or a "damsel in distress." Her level of industry and self motivation was far above what you might normally find. Her IQ too. The idea that an interesting and smart person might be failing bc of some simple missed piece of the puzzle is cat nip to an investor type. The conversation and dynamic was interesting, stressful, fulfilling. There seemed to be a genuine connection. I don't know if Tara found me interesting, but I had never met anyone like her. She wasn't an antagonist. She fell in love too it seemed and some of what you notice as a reader is similar to how BPD people are. They want love and success in relationship but having failed catastrophically in the past they plan for that inevitability. That split is something you can see. But I loved Tara and enjoyed every moment with her. That might be naieve but that is true.
