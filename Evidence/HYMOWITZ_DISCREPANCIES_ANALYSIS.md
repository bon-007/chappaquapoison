## DOCUMENT METADATA
- **Source:** Hymowitz_Discrepancies_Final.pdf
- **Pages:** 2
- **Type:** Forensic Evaluation Discrepancies / Declaration
- **Subject:** Analysis of two different versions of a document submitted to Dr. Paul Hymowitz (forensic psychiatrist)
- **Key Finding:** The document identifies intentional changes between an early draft (June 2018) and a later version (August 2019) that altered key factual claims

## EXTRACTED TEXT SUMMARY

### Part 1: Introduction (Page 1)
The document states:
- In discovery, an EARLIER VERSION of the document sent to Dr. Hymowitz was found
- This earlier version was sent BEFORE Tara sent it to Matan Gavish for edits
- Both versions contain falsehoods, but DIFFERENT falsehoods
- The second version was "obviously edited to attempt to mislead Dr. Hymowitz"

### Part 2: Side-by-Side Comparison (Page 1)

**EARLY VERSION (June 2018):**
"Tara was in the process of being sober (she was in AA) and starting up her dream business Tara Knoll (a handbag business.) She made it to the final rounds of Shark Tank for Tara Knoll. After a few dates with Steve, he pressured her to begin drinking alcohol again even though she felt great not drinking. The second time Tara had sex with Steve she did not remember it. She remembers getting drunk with him, then waking up unclothed in his bed in an apartment in NYC. She was then taken with Steve and his friend Chris Ochoa to go to the beach – whisked away quickly in the am - but it ended up being a whole weekend affair in the Hamptons – an overnight stay Tara was not told about. She remembers feeling used and just wanted to go home."

**LATER VERSION (August 2019):**
"I didn't particularly like Steve romantically, but he was very aggressive in pursuing me. One night we went out drinking with Chris (probably our third date). I drank way more than I was comfortable with and was not in the mood to hang out with Steve. In the morning, I woke up unclothed in his bed in his apartment in NYC. I don't remember the details of the previous night, which was very strange. Shortly after waking up, I was whiskéd to go "to the beach" with Steve and Chris. I resisted, but they were very aggressive, so I complied. Hours later we arrived at a home in the Hamptons. I was then told we would be staying the night there. At the time, I had a dog, Riley, I had to go back and feed. They said I had to stay – I called a friend and my sister to get me but they were busy. One of my friends went to feed and walk Riley. At the time, I felt used and uncomfortable, but for some reason I also thought I was interested in Steve. I guess this was the start of the abusive cycle with Steve."

### Part 3: Key Changes Identified (Pages 1-2)

**CHANGE #1: AA/Alcohol Recovery — Removed from Second Draft**

**Early Version Statement:**
- "Tara was in the process of being sober (she was in AA)"
- Presents her as in recovery and relapsing
- All blame for drinking again laid on Steve
- Portrays Tara as having "no self agency, no responsibility for her own actions"

**Later Version Statement:**
- AA reference completely deleted
- No mention of recovery status
- "Deliberately not discussed with Dr. Griffin, the court-ordered addiction specialist"
- Document states: "It seems that the second draft is attempting to present a better picture of Tara than that of an alcoholic"

**Claim:** This change obscures Tara's actual addiction history from the forensic evaluator.

---

**CHANGE #2: Sexual Encounter — Made "Significantly Worse" in Second Draft**

**Early Version Description:**
- "The second time Tara had sex with Steve she did not remember it"
- She describes drinking too much and blacking out
- Implies alcohol-related memory loss, not drugging

**Later Version Description:**
- "I woke up unclothed in his bed in his apartment in NYC"
- "I don't remember the details of the previous night, which was very strange"
- Combined with "he was very aggressive," this IMPLIES drugging
- Changes narrative from "I drank too much and blacked out" to "He may have drugged me"

**Claim:** The document argues the second version is deliberately edited to make the encounter sound non-consensual/potentially involving drugging, when the original account was consistent with alcohol-related blackout.

---

**CHANGE #3: Hamptons Trip and Riley the Dog — Material Facts Changed**

**Early Version Description:**
- "She was then taken with Steve and his friend Chris Ochoa to go to the beach – whisked away quickly in the am"
- "an overnight stay Tara was not told about"
- Implies she was taken against her will without planning
- No mention of the dog

**Later Version Description:**
- "I was whiskéd to go 'to the beach' with Steve and Chris. I resisted, but they were very aggressive, so I complied."
- "At the time, I had a dog, Riley, I had to go back and feed. They said I had to stay"
- "I called a friend and my sister to get me but they were busy. One of my friends went to feed and walk Riley"

**Key Claims About the Dog:**
1. Tara claims Riley was NOT at the Hamptons
2. She claims she had to stay against her will because Riley was home alone
3. Someone else had to go back to feed and walk Riley

**DOCUMENT EVIDENCE (Page 2):**

The Hymowitz document provides CONTRADICTORY EVIDENCE:
- "The following picture shows that isn't true. Tara's dog, Riley, was with them in the Hamptons."
- The photo caption: "He can be seen sitting on her lap in this Facebook post where they are feeding a turtle in the lake."
- This image is identified as showing: "Tara's dog, Riley, was with them in the Hamptons. He can be seen sitting on her lap in this Facebook post where they are feeding a turtle in the lake."
- Additional evidence: "In addition, Tara had packed a bag, as further evidence that in fact she wasn't whisked away without planning."

## NARRATIVE IMPACT ANALYSIS

### Core Argument of Hymowitz Document:

The document argues that Tara's account submitted to the forensic evaluator contains **INTENTIONAL ALTERATIONS** that:

1. **Hide addiction history** - Removes AA reference that would show relapse pattern
2. **Dramatize sexual encounter** - Changes narrative from "drank too much" to "possibly drugged"
3. **Create false coercion narrative** - Claims she was forced to Hamptons when she actually packed a bag and went voluntarily; claims Riley was home alone when he was present

**Conclusion of Document:** The presence of Riley in the turtle photo "disproves both" the claims that:
- Tara couldn't leave to feed Riley (because Riley was there)
- Tara was "whisked away" without planning (because she had packed a bag)
