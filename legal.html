The California judgment

On February 22, 2022, a San Francisco jury found Tara Walsh liable for battery, domestic violence, and intentional infliction of emotional distress. The punitive-damages form asked whether the conduct involved “malice, oppression, or fraud”; the jury answered yes. The separate $50,000 punitive award was later struck. The Second Amended Judgment, dated August 11, 2022, totaled $332,080.74 including costs. The judgment was affirmed on appeal on September 15, 2023.

The New York speech order and appeal

The December 3, 2021 order granting the Attorney for the Child's motion on default and the February 2, 2022 order reviewed on appeal are distinct. At the January 2022 hearing, Russell's counsel participated by making objections and cross-examining the mother.

On March 22, 2023, the Second Department held that the February order was not entered on default. It deleted the blanket direction to remove “any existing blogs and likenesses” and substituted a narrower requirement covering blogs that referenced the proceedings or disparaged the child's relatives, and the child's likenesses posted in connection with those blogs. The court upheld the remaining restrictions and affirmed the order insofar as appealed from. This decision did not vacate the custody award.

Reading the record

The evidence catalog brings together court records, testimony, correspondence, laboratory reports, published material, and the author's own accounts. Read each item in its stated context. An exhibit label or a place in the catalog does not, by itself, establish admission at trial, authentication, or a judicial finding.

The narrative preserves the author's account alongside the documentary record. Methodology and sources explains the reading approach. Contact the publisher with a correction and its source.
